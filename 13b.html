The rotor housings and side plates are glass, and the front side is cut away to show rotor 1; the rotors, apex seals, eccentric shaft and gears stay solid inside. Each rotor is a three-flank Wankel with a 105 mm generating radius and a 15 mm eccentricity, 80 mm wide, sweeping 654 cc; the pair displaces 1.3 litres at 9.0:1. The rotor ring gear has 51 teeth against a fixed 34-tooth stationary gear, so the rotors turn at one third of the eccentric shaft. The leading plug fires first, the trailing plug follows, and each rotor fires alternately every 180° of eccentric rotation. Blue and orange streams show the side-port intake and the peripheral exhaust; the amber mist is port injection. The two live cards are fixed in the drawing at rotor 1 face A; only their leaders follow the charge and spray.
